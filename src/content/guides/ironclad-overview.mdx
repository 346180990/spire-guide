The Ironclad is the first character most players encounter in Slay the Spire 2, and for good reason — his straightforward Strength scaling and self-sustain mechanics make him the easiest to pick up. But don't mistake accessibility for simplicity. The Ironclad has some of the deepest build variety in the game, from Demon Form-fueled one-shot kills to intricate exhaust combos that cycle your deck to zero cards.

## Core Mechanics

The Ironclad's starting relic, **Burning Blood**, heals 6 HP after every combat. This gives you a sustain cushion that lets you take more aggressive paths early — you can afford to trade HP for elite kills and card upgrades.

His card pool revolves around three main archetypes:

1. **Strength Scaling** — Stack Strength via Demon Form, Spot Weakness, and Inflame. Multiply it with multi-hit attacks like Heavy Blade, Sword Boomerang, and Twin Strike.
2. **Exhaust Synergy** — Exhaust cards to thin your deck, trigger Feel No Pain for block, and scale Dark Embrace for infinite draw. Corruption turns all skills into 0-cost exhaust fodder.
3. **Self-Damage / HP Manipulation** — Use Hemokinesis, Bloodletting, and Offering to convert HP into tempo, then heal back with Reaper and Feed.

## Starting Deck

The Ironclad starts with:

- 5x **Strike** (deal 6 damage)
- 4x **Defend** (gain 5 block)
- 1x **Bash** (deal 8 damage, apply 2 Vulnerable)

Your first priority is removing Strikes at shops and adding efficient attacks to replace Bash's role as your primary damage source.

## Best Relics

**Red Skull** — +3 Strength while below 50% HP. Enables aggressive self-damage lines and makes Hemokinesis nearly free.

**Snecko Eye** — Randomized costs with +2 draw per turn. The Ironclad has the most 2+ cost cards in the game, so Snecko turns average draws into explosive turns.

**Dead Branch** — Every exhausted card generates a random card. With Corruption active, you create an endless stream of free skills.

## Early Game Priorities

1. **Act 1** — Take 2-3 strong attacks (Carnage, Uppercut, Hemokinesis). Fight at least 2 elites — the Ironclad's sustain makes this safer than for other characters. Upgrade Bash at the first campfire.
2. **Act 2** — Commit to your archetype. By floor 20 you should know whether you're Strength, Exhaust, or hybrid. Pick relics that support your direction.
3. **Act 3** — Scale or die. Act 3 enemies and bosses have massive HP pools — you need a plan to multiply your damage. Strength builds need Limit Break; exhaust builds need Corruption + Dark Embrace online.

## Common Mistakes

- **Over-picking attacks early** — You don't need 8 attacks by floor 10. 3-4 good attacks plus Bash is enough. Dead draws lose runs.
- **Ignoring exhaust cards** — True Grit and Burning Pact look weak on their own, but they enable your best combos. Don't skip them.
- **Forcing an archetype** — Not every run gets Demon Form. Sometimes you win with a lean Anger/Rampage deck. Adapt to what you're offered.

## Summary

The Ironclad rewards aggressive play and smart HP management. His best runs turn your life total into a resource you spend for tempo, then reclaim through sustain. Start with Strength builds to learn the character, then explore exhaust engines once you understand card evaluation.
