Curseborne Power is the Necrobinder's highest-ceiling build. Where other characters avoid Curses at all costs, you actively seek them out — each Curse in your deck becomes a resource. Cursed Key gives +1 energy per Curse. Forbidden Tome draws for every Curse played. What looks like deck pollution to other characters is pure fuel for the Necrobinder.

## Build Overview

The build operates on a simple principle: Curses are bad for normal characters, so their effects are overtuned to compensate. You exploit this by converting Curse downsides into upsides. Accursed Pact adds 3 random Curses to your deck and deals 35 damage. Corrupted Offering lets you play a Curse for free and draws 3 cards. By Act 3, your deck might have 6-8 Curses — and be stronger for it.

The risk: Curses still have negative effects. If your synergy pieces aren't in play, Curses are just dead draws that hurt you.

## Core Cards

**Accursed Pact** — Deal 35 damage. Shuffle 3 random Curses into your draw pile. The base damage is overtuned because Curses are "supposed" to be bad. In this build, adding 3 Curses is a benefit, not a cost. Upgrade to 50 damage.

**Hex Bolt** — Deal 18 damage. If the target has Doom, add a random Curse to your hand. Your early-game Curse generator and damage dealer. The Curse goes to your hand, not your draw pile — you can play it immediately with Corrupted Offering.

**Corrupted Offering** — Play a Curse from your hand for free, then draw 3 cards. Converts the worst card in your hand into 3 fresh draws. Makes Curses actively good to draw. Draft multiple copies.

**Vile Ritual** — Exhaust a Curse from your hand. Deal 9 damage to all enemies for each Curse in your discard pile. Your late-game finisher. With 6 Curses in discard, that's 54 AoE damage. Upgrade for 12 damage per Curse.

**Madness Stone** — For every Curse in your deck, gain 2 Strength at the start of combat. A deck with 6 Curses starts at +12 Strength. Your attacks hit like trucks from turn 1.

**Forbidden Tome** — Whenever you play a Curse, draw 1 card. Turns every Curse into a cantrip. With Corrupted Offering playing Curses for free, this creates draw chains where one Curse → draw → another Curse → draw.

## Key Relics

**Cursed Key** — +1 energy per Curse in your deck. A deck with 5 Curses has 8 energy per turn. You can play your entire hand every turn. The single most important relic for this build.

**Darkstone Periapt** — +6 max HP for every Curse in your deck. 6 Curses = +36 max HP. Makes you absurdly tanky, which covers the turns where you draw multiple Curses without payoff cards.

**Twisted Totem** — The first Curse played each turn has its negative effect negated. A Curse that would damage you or give you Weak is just a free card.

**Doomsday Clock** — Doom triggers twice per turn. If your Curses apply Doom (many do), this doubles your passive damage output.

**Soul Jar** — Harvested souls provide double bonus. Your Doom and Curse kills generate enhanced souls for permanent buffs.

## Act Strategy

### Act 1
**Goal:** Draft Hex Bolt and Accursed Pact. Start building Curse density.

Pick priority: Hex Bolt > Accursed Pact > Bone Shard > Soul Tap > Shadow Strike.

Take 1-2 Curses by the first Elite. Don't go overboard — 3 Curses with no payoff cards is still bad. The ratio should be roughly 1 payoff card per 2 Curses.

### Act 2
**Goal:** Add Corrupted Offering and Forbidden Tome. Your Curse count should be 4-6 by the boss.

Pick priority: Corrupted Offering > Forbidden Tome > Vile Ritual > Accursed Pact (second copy).

At this point Curses stop being a downside. Every Curse you draw becomes a resource for Corrupted Offering.

### Act 3
**Goal:** Add Madness Stone for flat Strength scaling. Your deck should have 6-8 Curses.

Pick priority: Madness Stone > Vile Ritual > Accursed Pact > Soul Reap.

Madness Stone is a late-game pickup — you need Curse density first. A Madness Stone with 2 Curses is +4 Strength. With 8 Curses it's +16.

## Common Pitfalls

**Curses without payoff cards** — This is the #1 way this build dies. A deck with 5 Curses and no Corrupted Offering or Cursed Key is a deck full of dead draws. Build the payoff first, then add Curses.

**Too many Curses, no draw** — Drawing 3 Curses in one turn when you can only play one is a dead turn. Corrupted Offering and Forbidden Tome fix this, but you need enough copies.

**Ignoring Curse downsides** — Some Curses deal damage, apply debuffs, or exhaust your other cards. Twisted Totem and Darkstone Periapt mitigate these, but you still need to know what each Curse does.

**Vile Ritual too early** — With 1 Curse in discard, Vile Ritual deals 9 AoE damage. That's terrible. Wait until you have 4+ Curses cycling through your deck before adding this finisher.
