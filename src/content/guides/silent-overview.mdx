The Silent is Slay the Spire 2's precision killer. Where the Ironclad smashes through problems with raw Strength, the Silent dismantles enemies through poison accumulation, card manipulation, and overwhelming tempo from 0-cost attacks. She has the highest skill ceiling of the starter characters — a well-piloted Silent deck can feel completely untouchable.

## Core Mechanics

The Silent's starting relic, **Ring of the Snake**, draws 2 additional cards at the start of your first turn. This extra velocity gives you more options on the most important turn of every fight — the one where you set up your engine. It also makes the Silent the best character for leveraging card draw synergies.

Three major archetypes define the Silent's card pool:

1. **Poison** — Stack poison with Deadly Poison, Noxious Fumes, and Crippling Cloud. Multiply it with Catalyst. Block while enemies die to damage-over-time. The safest, most consistent Silent archetype.
2. **Shiv** — Generate 0-cost Shivs via Blade Dance, Cloak and Dagger, and Infinite Blades. Scale them with Accuracy, Wrist Blade, and after-image effects. Overwhelm enemies with sheer card volume.
3. **Discard** — Cycle your deck with Prepared, Acrobatics, and Calculated Gamble. Trigger discard payoffs like Eviscerate and Sneaky Strike. Harder to pilot but generates absurd value once online.

## Starting Deck

The Silent starts with:
- 5x **Strike** (deal 6 damage)
- 4x **Defend** (gain 5 block)
- 1x **Neutralize** (deal 3 damage, apply 1 Weak)

Neutralize is quietly one of the best starter cards in the game. Weak reduces enemy damage by 25%, and for 0 energy. Upgrade it early — 2 Weak for 0 energy is premium mitigation through all three acts.

## Best Relics

**Wrist Blade** — All 0-cost attacks (which means every Shiv) deal +4 damage. Transforms Blade Dance from "good value" to "primary damage plan." Stack Accuracy on top and you're hitting for 10+ per Shiv.

**Tough Bandages** — Every time you discard a card, gain 3 block. With Calculated Gamble+ you can discard your entire hand and generate 20-30 block for 0 energy. Enables discard decks to completely skip block cards in the late game.

**The Specimen** — When a poisoned enemy dies, transfer all its poison to a random enemy. In multi-enemy fights, this chains kills into kills. With Catalyst, you can stack 100+ poison on one target and watch it cascade through the entire room.

## Act-by-Act Strategy

### Act 1
Prioritize front-loaded damage. The Silent struggles against Act 1 elites without 2-3 solid attack cards — Dagger Throw, Poison Stab, and Dash are early MVPs. Avoid taking too many setup cards (Tools of the Trade, Well-Laid Plans) until you have damage sorted out.

### Act 2
This is where the Silent shines. By now you should have your archetype online. Poison decks want Catalyst and Noxious Fumes. Shiv decks need Accuracy and after-image relics. Discard decks come together with Calculated Gamble and Reflex.

### Act 3
Scaling becomes the priority. The Silent's boss-killing ability depends on multiplying your damage — Catalyst for poison, Accuracy stacking for shivs, Eviscerate for discard. If you don't have scaling by the Act 3 boss, you'll lose to HP bloat.

## Common Mistakes

- **Over-valuing setup powers**: Tools of the Trade, Infinite Blades, and Noxious Fumes are good — but taking three of them before you have block or damage gets you killed by Act 1 elites.
- **Ignoring Weak**: Neutralize is your best card. Malaise is a run-winner against multi-hit enemies (Book of Stabbing, Hexaghost). Weak is block that doesn't cost a card slot.
- **Hybrid builds without purpose**: Mixing one shiv card and one poison card and one discard card isn't synergy — it's a pile. Pick one direction and commit.

## Summary

The Silent rewards patience and planning. She's weakest on turn 1 and strongest on turn 6 — set up your engine, then watch enemies crumble to a thousand cuts (or one very large poison tick). Poison is the recommended starting archetype because it demands less card evaluation skill. Graduate to shivs and discard once you understand enemy patterns.
