Minion Sacrifice is the Necrobinder's most visceral build — you summon undead minions, let them absorb damage and deal chip damage for a few turns, then sacrifice them for devastating burst effects. Each minion death triggers Rotting Giant's block scaling. Each sacrifice heals you and draws cards. The build plays like a horror movie where you're the monster.

## Build Overview

The gameplay loop: Raise Dead puts a 10 HP zombie into play → the zombie tanks hits and deals 4 damage per turn → when it's low on HP, Sacrifice it for 25 damage and 10 block → Rotting Giant gains permanent scaling from the death → Death March refills your board with fresh minions.

Unlike the Regent's allies, Necrobinder minions are disposable. You don't protect them — you use them up.

## Core Cards

**Raise Dead** — Summon a Zombie with 10 HP. Deals 4 damage per turn. Costs 1. Your basic summon. Draft 2-3 copies. The Zombie is fragile but efficient — it'll take 1-2 hits and deal 4-8 damage before dying or being sacrificed.

**Skeletal Horde** — Summon 3 Skeletons. Each has 5 HP and deals 3 damage per turn. Costs 3. Best when you have an empty board. Three bodies means three sacrifice targets and nine passive damage per turn.

**Sacrifice** — Destroy a minion. Deal damage equal to its remaining HP × 3. If the minion had 8 HP, deal 24 damage. Your primary finisher. Upgrade for ×4 multiplier.

**Bone Barrier** — Destroy a minion. Gain block equal to its max HP. A 10-HP Zombie gives 10 block. A 20-HP Rotting Giant gives 20 block. Emergency defense on demand.

**Rotting Giant** — Summon a Giant minion with 30 HP. Whenever ANY minion dies, gain +3 block and the Giant gains +2 damage permanently. The more minions you sacrifice, the stronger the Giant becomes. Your scaling engine.

**Death March** — All minions attack immediately, then summon 2 Zombies. Triggers every minion's attack out of sequence for a burst damage turn. The 2 fresh Zombies refill your board for the next cycle.

## Key Relics

**Phylactery** — The first minion sacrificed each combat is revived next turn. Free minion every fight. Sacrifice your best minion, get it back for free.

**Grave Dirt** — Summoned minions have +5 HP. Zombies go from 10 to 15 HP. More HP = more damage when sacrificed = more block from Bone Barrier.

**Sacrificial Blade** — Whenever you sacrifice a minion, gain 1 Strength for the rest of combat. With 4 sacrifices per fight, that's +4 Strength compounding with your attacks.

**Soul Jar** — Harvested souls provide double bonus. Minion kills in elite fights generate souls with double the buff value.

**Necromancer's Ring** — Start combat with a Zombie already in play. Free sacrifice target from turn 1.

## Act Strategy

### Act 1
**Goal:** Draft Raise Dead and at least one Sacrifice effect. Build the summon-sacrifice loop.

Pick priority: Raise Dead > Sacrifice > Bone Shard > Soul Tap > Shadow Strike.

One Raise Dead and one Sacrifice gets you through Act 1. The Zombie tanks hits while you chip with attacks. Sacrifice it when it's at 3-4 HP for maximum value.

### Act 2
**Goal:** Add Skeletal Horde and Rotting Giant. Establish the scaling engine.

Pick priority: Rotting Giant > Skeletal Horde > Bone Barrier > Raise Dead (more copies).

Rotting Giant is your Act 2 priority. The earlier you get it, the more minion deaths it scales from. A Giant that's been in play since floor 1 of Act 2 will have +20 damage by the boss.

### Act 3
**Goal:** Add Death March and finishers. Deck should have 6-8 summon/sacrifice cards.

Pick priority: Death March > Sacrifice (upgrade) > Bone Barrier > Raise Dead.

Death March is your boss killer. With 5 minions on board, Death March triggers all their attacks (that's 20-30 damage) and summons 2 fresh Zombies for the next cycle.

## Common Pitfalls

**Sacrificing at full HP** — Don't sacrifice a full-HP Zombie. Let it tank, deal damage, and drop to 2-4 HP, then sacrifice it. The damage multiplier (×3 or ×4) only matters on remaining HP — but you waste the minion's passive damage if you sacrifice immediately.

**Empty board, no summons in hand** — If all your minions die and you have no Raise Dead, you're taking full damage to face. Keep at least one summon card in hand at all times.

**Rotting Giant dying** — The Giant has 30 HP, which seems like a lot, but enemies focus the largest minion. Protect it with Bone Barrier or keep a backup summon. Losing a fully-scaled Giant is devastating.

**Over-reliance on Death March** — Death March is a burst card, not a build-around. If it's your only way to trigger minions, you're too slow. Have Sacrifice and Bone Barrier for non-March turns.
