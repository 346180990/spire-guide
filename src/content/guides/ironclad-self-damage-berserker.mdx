The Self-Damage Berserker turns the Ironclad's HP bar into a weapon. Every point of self-inflicted damage becomes Strength via Rupture, block via Self-Forming Clay, and card advantage via Offering. This is the highest-risk, highest-reward Ironclad archetype — you'll often hover at 20-30 HP while dealing triple-digit damage.

## Build Overview

The engine is a three-card combo: use Offering to draw and gain energy, take self-damage from Hemokinesis or Combust to trigger Rupture's Strength gain, then close fights with Blood for Blood (which costs 0 after enough self-damage). Self-Forming Clay and Burning Blood keep you alive through the self-inflicted damage.

The build needs careful HP management — every fight is a balancing act between spending HP for power and preserving enough to survive.

## Core Cards

**Rupture** — The centerpiece. Every time you lose HP from a card, gain 1 Strength. Upgrade to 2 Strength per trigger. Priority upgrade.

**Offering** — Lose 6 HP, draw 5 cards, gain 2 energy. The best draw card in the game. The HP loss is a feature, not a cost — it triggers Rupture.

**Hemokinesis** — Deal 18 damage, lose 3 HP. Repeatable self-damage source that also hits hard. Scales with Strength.

**Blood for Blood** — Costs 4, but its cost decreases by 1 for each time you lose HP in combat. In this deck it's often free by turn 3. Deals 22 damage.

**Brutality** — Lose 1 HP per turn, draw 1 extra card each turn. Passive Rupture trigger every single turn. The chip damage adds up — have sustain ready.

**Combust** — Lose 1 HP per turn, deal 7 damage to all enemies. Another passive Rupture enabler that also provides AoE. Pairs well with multiple Ruptures.

## Key Relics

**Self-Forming Clay** — Core defensive relic. You gain block equal to the HP you lose. Every Offering gives 6 block. Every Hemokinesis gives 3 block. Turns self-damage into a block engine.

**Burning Blood** — Your starter relic heals 6 HP after combat. In this archetype, that healing is essential sustain.

**Tungsten Rod** — Reduces all HP loss by 1. Cuts Brutality's chip damage in half. Makes Combust free. Increases your margin for error enormously.

**Magic Flower** — Healing is 50% more effective. Burning Blood heals 9 instead of 6. Reaper with high Strength full-heals in one swing.

**Red Skull** — +3 Strength when below 50% HP. You'll be below 50% HP most of the time — this is permanently active.

## Act Strategy

### Act 1
**Goal:** Draft Rupture and a self-damage source before the boss. Skip Hemokinesis if you don't have Rupture yet.

Pick priority: Rupture > Offering > Hemokinesis > Cleave > Anger > Clothesline.

Upgrade Rupture first. The difference between 1 and 2 Strength per trigger is build-defining.

Fight elites cautiously — you can take Gremlin Nob if you have Hemokinesis. Avoid Lagavulin without Rupture; the fight is too long without scaling.

### Act 2
**Goal:** Add Blood for Blood or Combust as a finisher. Layer multiple self-damage sources.

Pick priority: Blood for Blood > Combust > Brutality > Disarm > Flame Barrier > Reaper.

Combust solves the AoE fights that punish single-target builds. Reaper with Strength heals you from dangerous HP totals.

### Act 3
**Goal:** Add draw consistency and defensive tools. Your damage should already be solved.

Pick priority: Offering (second copy) > Reaper > Impervious > Battle Trance > Disarm.

Time Offering carefully — the HP loss matters more in long boss fights. Against the final boss, prioritize getting two Ruptures in play before going aggressive.

## Common Pitfalls

**Rupture without self-damage** — Taking Rupture before you have Offering or Hemokinesis leaves you with a dead card. One self-damage source first, then Rupture.

**Too much self-damage, not enough sustain** — Brutality + Combust + Offering all firing means you lose 8+ HP per turn. You need Burning Blood, Reaper, or Self-Forming Clay online before stacking multiple passive damage sources.

**Blood for Blood too early** — It costs 4 and does nothing special without HP loss triggers. Draft Hemokinesis or Combust first, then pick Blood for Blood when it's already discounted.

**Playing Offering at low HP** — Below 20 HP, Offering can kill you. Wait for a block turn or a Reaper before spending that 6 HP. An upgraded Offering is still not worth dying for.
