The Command Chain build is the most straightforward way to play Regent — you summon a small team of allies, then issue Commands every turn to trigger their bonus effects. A well-piloted Command Chain generates more value per energy than any other build in the game. The catch: if your allies die, you're left with expensive blanks.

## Build Overview

The Command mechanic is Regent's defining feature. Each ally has a passive bonus and a Command trigger — when you play a Command card targeting that ally, the trigger fires. The build focuses on assembling a tight squad of 2-3 reliable allies and packing enough Command cards to trigger them every turn.

The ideal sequence: play an ally → next turn, Rally them → the Rally triggers their Command bonus → follow with Coordinated Strike for the finisher.

## Core Cards

**Rally** — The basic Command. Target ally gains +3 damage and attacks immediately. Your bread and butter. Draft multiple copies. Upgrade for +5 damage and draw 1 card.

**Battle Orders** — Target ally gains 8 block and attacks. The defensive Command that keeps allies alive through hallway fights. Crucial for longer fights where ally HP matters.

**Coordinated Strike** — Deal 12 damage. Then trigger the nearest ally's Command for free. This card effectively plays itself + a free Rally. Your highest-value single card. Upgrade to 18 damage.

**Formation** — Play the next two Commands in your hand for 0 cost. Sets up devastating Command chains where Formation → Battle Orders → Rally triggers three ally effects in one turn.

**Warcry** — All allies gain +2 damage this turn and attack. Mass Command for wide boards. Better with 3+ allies, but still decent with 2.

**Banner of Courage** — Legacy card. For the rest of combat, your first Command each turn costs 0. The tempo swing from free Commands every turn is game-winning.

## Key Relics

**War Banner** — Allies deal +3 damage. Every summon, every Command attack, every Coordinated Strike — all buffed.

**Signet Ring** — First Command each turn costs 0. Stacks with Banner of Courage for potentially 2 free Commands per turn.

**Signal Whistle** — Commanding an ally draws a card. Solves the build's biggest weakness: running out of gas after your Command chain.

**Crown of Command** — Commands trigger twice on the first target each turn. Double Rally means +6 damage and two attacks from one card.

**Sealed Testament** — Legacy effects are 50% stronger. Banner of Courage now reduces Command cost by 2 instead of 1.

## Act Strategy

### Act 1
**Goal:** Draft Squire and at least two Command cards. Build the foundation.

Pick priority: Squire > Rally > Shield Bearer > Coordinated Strike > Holy Lancer > Strike.

Upgrade Rally first. A free draw on Rally makes a huge difference in cycling through your deck.

Fight elites with Squire on board for chip block. Your starting deck is decent for Act 1 because Squire provides free value every turn.

### Act 2
**Goal:** Add Battle Orders for ally protection and Coordinated Strike for damage.

Pick priority: Coordinated Strike > Battle Orders > Formation > Holy Lancer > Bodyguard.

Protect allies with Bodyguard. Losing a Knight Commander to chip damage is how Command Chain runs die.

### Act 3
**Goal:** Add Banner of Courage and Formation. Your Command density should be 40%+ of your deck.

Pick priority: Banner of Courage > Formation > Warcry > Knight Commander > Ancient Decree.

Ancient Decree is a Legacy card that makes all Commands cost 1 less for the combat. Combined with Banner of Courage, all Commands are free. You play your entire hand every turn.

## Common Pitfalls

**Too many allies, too few Commands** — 4 allies and 2 Commands means most allies sit idle. The ratio should be roughly 1:2 (ally to Command).

**Ally dies without backup** — If your only Squire dies and you have no other ally in hand, your entire Command hand becomes dead cards. Have 2-3 allies in the deck minimum.

**Ignoring defensive Commands** — Battle Orders and Shield Bearer aren't flashy, but they keep your allies alive. A dead ally provides zero Command value.

**Formation without setup** — Playing Formation with no Commands in hand does literally nothing. Check your hand before playing it.
