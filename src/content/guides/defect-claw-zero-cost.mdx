Claw is the Defect's most polarizing card. It's a 0-cost Attack that deals 3 damage and increases the damage of ALL Claws by 2 for the rest of combat. Draft five Claws, cycle your thin deck every turn with Scrape and All for One, and by turn 5 each Claw deals 30+ damage for 0 energy. This build turns the Defect into an aggro character — no orbs, no Focus, just claws.

## Build Overview

The Claw cycle build abandons the Defect's traditional orb scaling in favor of a razor-thin deck that plays every card every turn. All for One is the key recursion tool — it returns all 0-cost cards from your discard pile to your hand. Scrape draws 3 cards but only keeps 0-cost ones. With enough Claws and support, you play 10+ cards per turn for 0 energy.

The build demands aggressive deck thinning. Strikes are garbage. Defends are acceptable. Every card that isn't 0-cost needs to justify its existence.

## Core Cards

**Claw** — 0 cost, 3 damage, +2 to all Claws for the rest of combat. The damage compounds. With 4 Claws: first Claw = 3, second = 5, third = 7, fourth = 9. Next turn they start at 11 and climb from there. Draft every copy. Upgrade for 5 base damage.

**Scrape** — Draw 3 cards, discard any that cost more than 0. In a deck of mostly 0-cost cards, Scrape reads "Draw 3." The discard is a feature — it puts cards into your discard pile for All for One to recover. Upgrade for Draw 4.

**All for One** — Deal 15 damage, return all 0-cost cards from your discard pile to your hand. This is how you play 10 Claws in one turn. Costs 2 — make it your priority upgrade target (reduces to 1).

**Go for the Eyes** — 0 cost, apply 1 Weak. If the enemy is attacking, apply 2 Weak instead. Free defense that also triggers Shuriken/Kunai for playing attacks.

**FTL** — 0 cost, deal 7 damage, draw 1 card if you've played 3+ cards this turn. Play it 3rd or later in your sequence for a free draw. Upgrade for 10 damage.

**Reboot** — Shuffle your discard pile into your draw pile, then draw 4 cards. Emergency refill when you run out of 0-cost cards. Also triggers Sundial and Abacus on shuffle.

## Key Relics

**Unceasing Top** — When your hand is empty, draw a card. In a 0-cost deck you empty your hand every turn. This relic means infinite draw.

**Shuriken** — Every 3 attacks played in a turn, gain 1 Strength. You'll play 10+ attacks per turn. Permanent Strength scaling.

**Kunai** — Every 3 attacks played in a turn, gain 1 Dexterity. Your block cards get better every turn. Combos with Shuriken since both trigger on attacks.

**Ornamental Fan** — Every 3 attacks played in a turn, gain 4 block. Free defense while doing your Claw sequence.

**Art of War** — If you play no Attacks during your turn, gain 1 extra energy next turn. Counter-intuitive for this build but useful on setup turns.

## Act Strategy

### Act 1
**Goal:** Draft Claws aggressively. Survive Elites with whatever you can find.

Pick priority: Claw > Ball Lightning > Scrape > Go for the Eyes > FTL > Cold Snap.

Take 2-3 Claws. One Claw is not a build — you need at least 3 for the scaling to matter. Ball Lightning carries you through Act 1 until you have critical mass.

### Act 2
**Goal:** Find All for One. Add more Claws. Remove Strikes at every shop.

Pick priority: All for One > Claw (more copies) > Scrape > Reboot > Go for the Eyes > FTL.

Shop priority: remove Strikes > buy Claw if offered > buy Kunai/Shuriken.

### Act 3
**Goal:** Deck should be 15-20 cards. Thin to perfection. Add finishers.

Pick priority: All for One (second copy) > Reboot > Madness > Beam Cell > Reprogram.

Reprogram gives +1 Strength and +1 Dexterity per play. In a 0-cost cycling deck, you can play Reprogram 3+ times per fight for huge scaling.

## Common Pitfalls

**One Claw is not a build** — Taking one Claw and expecting it to work is the #1 mistake. You need 3+ Claws or the scaling is too slow.

**Keeping Strikes** — A Strike is a dead draw that costs 1 energy and breaks the 0-cost cycle. Remove every Strike. Defends are acceptable early but should be removed by Act 3.

**All for One at 2 cost** — An unupgraded All for One costs 2 in a deck where most turns generate 3 energy. That's crippling. Upgrade it immediately or don't play it.

**Too many non-zero-cost cards** — Every card that costs 1+ is a card Scrape will discard and All for One won't retrieve. The golden ratio: 80% of your deck should cost 0.
