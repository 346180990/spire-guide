The Defect is Slay the Spire 2's scaling engine — an automaton that starts each fight modest but snowballs into an unstoppable force through orb channeling and passive damage. Where the Ironclad and Silent play cards for immediate effect, the Defect builds infrastructure. Every orb is an investment that pays out turn after turn, and every Power card is a brick in an ever-growing wall.

## Core Mechanics

The Defect's starting relic, **Cracked Core**, channels 1 Lightning Orb at the start of each combat. Orbs are the Defect's defining mechanic — passive effects that sit in orb slots (3 base, expandable to 10) and trigger each turn:

1. **Lightning** — Deals 3 (passive) / 8 (evoke) damage to a random enemy. Your bread-and-butter orb for hallway fights.
2. **Frost** — Gains 2 (passive) / 5 (evoke) block. Once you have 3+ Frost orbs, you're effectively unkillable against most enemies.
3. **Dark** — Accumulates 6 damage per turn. When evoked, it unleashes ALL stored damage on the lowest-HP enemy. Boss-killing tool — let it sit for 4+ turns, then dual-cast for 70+ damage.
4. **Plasma** — Grants 1 energy per turn (passive) / 2 energy (evoke). Turns energy into a non-issue, enabling massive Power chains.

**Focus** is the Defect's scaling stat — each point of Focus increases all orb effectiveness by 1 (Lightning +1 damage, Frost +1 block, Dark +6 stored per turn). A Defect with 5+ Focus is playing a different game from everyone else.

## Starting Deck

The Defect starts with:
- 4x **Strike** (deal 6 damage)
- 4x **Defend** (gain 5 block)
- 1x **Dualcast** (evoke your first orb twice for 0 energy)
- 1x **Zap** (channel 1 Lightning for 0 energy)

Dualcast is your early damage plan. Evoking a Lightning orb on turn 1 deals 16 damage for 0 energy — enough to end most Act 1 hallway fights in 2 turns. Upgrade Zap early (0 cost → 0 cost with "channel 3 Lightning") for faster orb filling.

## Best Relics

**Data Disk** — Start each combat with +1 Focus. Simple, universal, and stacks with everything. The Defect's equivalent of Vajra — unexciting but correct.

**Runic Capacitor** — Gain +3 orb slots (6 total). More slots mean more passive value per turn and bigger evoke chains. Essential for Frost turtle strategies.

**Gold-Plated Cables** — Your rightmost orb's passive effect is doubled. Positioning micro becomes a significant skill test — put Frost there for block, Lightning for damage.

**Inserter** — Every 2 turns, gain 1 orb slot (boss relic). Solves the Defect's biggest problem — limited scaling ceiling. By turn 6 of a boss fight, you have 6-7 orb slots and the fight is effectively over.

## Act-by-Act Strategy

### Act 1
Survive and collect orb generation. Ball Lightning, Cold Snap, and Coolheaded are your best early picks — they channel orbs AND provide immediate value. Fight 2 elites if possible — the Defect's passive orb damage handles Sentries and Lagavulin well. Avoid Gremlin Nob unless you've found significant direct damage.

### Act 2
Commit to Focus. Defragment and Biased Cognition are the highest-priority cards in the entire card pool. Biased Cognition into an Artifact source (Core Surge, Panacea) gives permanent +4/+5 Focus — this is the Defect's equivalent of a turn-1 Demon Form. Add Frost orb generation to stabilize.

### Act 3
Scale to infinity. By now you should have 6+ orb slots, 4+ Focus, and a mix of Frost and Lightning/Dark orbs. Creative AI generates random Powers every turn, which with enough energy can create unbeatable board states. Echo Form doubles your first card each turn — with it, you can play Defragment twice, channel 4 orbs in one turn, or double-evoke for 100+ Dark damage.

## Common Mistakes

- **Ignoring Frost**: The Defect has poor upfront block. Without Frost orbs, you'll bleed HP through every Act 2 hallway fight. One Cold Snap changes everything.
- **Taking too many orb types**: A deck with Lightning, Frost, Dark, AND Plasma is a deck that does nothing well. Pick one damage type (Lightning or Dark) and commit.
- **Over-valuing Capacitor without Focus**: 6 orb slots with 0 Focus generates 12 damage/block per turn. 3 orb slots with 5 Focus generates 24+. Focus first, slots second.
- **Biased Cognition without artifact**: -1 Focus per turn looks scary, but the fight should end before it matters. Don't skip Biased Cognition — it's the Defect's best card.

## Summary

The Defect rewards system-building and patience. You're constructing a machine that generates passive value every turn — the longer the fight, the more your advantage compounds. Against hallway fights, 2-3 Lightning orbs end things fast. Against bosses, 5 Frost orbs + 1 Dark orb + Focus scaling is the most reliable kill pattern in the game. Start with the Orb-Focus build and branch out once you understand orb micro.
