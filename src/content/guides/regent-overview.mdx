The Regent is Slay the Spire 2's tactical commander — a battlefield general who doesn't fight alone. Her unique Command system summons allies to the front line, her Legacy effects persist across turns and even across combat, and her playstyle rewards sequencing and foresight more than any other character. A well-commanded Regent army makes the Spire feel like a parade ground.

## Core Mechanics

The Regent's starting relic, **Royal Seal**, gives you a Command point at the start of each combat. The Command system is her defining mechanic:

1. **Command Points** — A secondary resource (like energy) spent to issue orders to allies. You generate Command through cards and retain it between turns. Maximum 5 Command.
2. **Allies** — Cards that summon units with HP, attack values, and special abilities. Allies persist in play until they die — they're not removed at end of turn. You can have up to 3 allies active simultaneously.
3. **Legacy Effects** — Buffs and passives that persist across turns AND across combat within the same act. If you play "Inspiring Speech" (all allies gain +2 Strength) in fight 1 of Act 2, that +2 Strength stays on every ally you summon for the rest of the act. Legacy is the Regent's snowball mechanic — stack enough of it and your army starts each fight already buffed.

Three major archetypes define the Regent's card pool:

1. **Star Engine** — Generate massive Command through Star-aligned cards. Spend Command on elite allies and devastating Order attacks. The most consistent Regent archetype, named for the star symbol shared across its key cards.
2. **Rally Commander** — Flood the field with cheap allies, then play Rally effects that buff your entire army at once. Swarm-and-scale — fragile early, unstoppable once the engine is online.
3. **Legacy Stacking** — Prioritize Legacy cards that accumulate permanent buffs. By the Act 3 boss, your allies enter play with +6 Strength, +4 Dexterity, and 10 bonus max HP from Legacy effects accumulated over the entire run.

## Starting Deck

The Regent starts with:
- 4x **Strike** (deal 6 damage)
- 4x **Defend** (gain 5 block)
- 1x **Call to Arms** (gain 2 Command, draw 1 card)
- 1x **Royal Guard** (summon a Guard ally: 8 HP, deals 4 damage per turn, blocks 3 per turn)

Royal Guard is your early-game anchor. For 1 energy, you get a permanent blocker/attacker that generates value every turn until it dies. Protect it, buff it, and it will carry you through Act 1.

## Best Relics

**Battle Standard** — Allies deal +2 damage. Simple, universal damage boost that multiplies across 3 active allies.

**War Council** — At the start of each combat, gain 1 Command and draw 1 additional card. Accelerates your setup turn — the Regent's biggest weakness is slow starts.

**Crown of Legacy** — Your Legacy effects also apply to YOU (not just allies). Inspiring Speech gives YOU +2 Strength. Rally to Me gives YOU +3 Dexterity. Transforms defensive Legacy cards into personal survival tools.

**General's Baton** — Issue 2 Orders per turn instead of 1 (boss relic). Orders are powerful commands that tell allies to attack or defend — doubling them doubles your effective damage output.

## Act-by-Act Strategy

### Act 1
Summon early, protect your allies. Royal Guard + 1-2 additional ally cards (Squire, Archer) give you a board that handles hallway fights with minimal input. Upgrade Call to Arms — the extra Command generation is essential. Fight elites cautiously — losing an ally to Gremlin Nob hurts more than losing HP.

### Act 2
Transition from "summon and forget" to "command and conquer." Add Command generators and Order cards. Star Engine comes online with cards like Astronomical Chart (gain 3 Command) and Precision Strike (spend 2 Command: ally attacks for double damage). Add your first Legacy cards — even one legacy buff per act compounds dramatically.

### Act 3
Your army should enter each fight already buffed from Legacy stacking. Focus on protecting your allies and scaling further. Marching Orders (all allies attack, then gain +2 Strength as Legacy) turns every fight into a Legacy farming opportunity. For bosses, build toward the ultimate play: three elite allies with 4+ Legacy buffs each, hitting for 25+ damage per turn passively while you defend.

## Common Mistakes

- **Letting allies die unnecessarily**: An ally at 1 HP still deals full damage. Block for them. The AI targets your lowest-HP ally first — position matters.
- **Ignoring Command generation**: Without Command, you can't issue Orders, and Order cards are dead draws. Aim for 2+ Command per turn from passive sources.
- **Legacy greed**: Taking 4 Legacy cards when you have 2 allies means none of your allies survive long enough to benefit. Allies first, Legacy second.
- **Over-filling the board**: 3 ally slots means you should plan around 2 permanent allies + 1 flex slot. Summoning a 4th ally forces you to dismiss one — wasted energy.

## Summary

The Regent is the most strategic character in Slay the Spire 2. She rewards long-term planning through Legacy effects, tactical positioning through ally management, and resource balancing through Command. She's weakest on turn 1 and strongest after turn 5, when your army is deployed and buffed. The Star Engine build is the recommended starting point — it teaches Command management and ally positioning without the complexity of Legacy stacking. Graduate to Legacy builds once you can consistently reach Act 3 with 2+ allies surviving every fight.
