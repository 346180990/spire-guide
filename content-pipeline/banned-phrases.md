# Banned AI-Voice Phrases

This document lists phrases and patterns that signal AI-generated text. During human review, search for these and replace them with natural alternatives.

## Banned Phrases (remove or rewrite)

| Banned Phrase | Why It's AI | Natural Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "delve into" | AI hallmark #1, never used by gamers | "dig into", "break down", or just start the sentence |
| "in the world of" | Overly formal, video-game-essay cliché | Just name the game directly |
| "it is important to note" | Padding. If it's important, just say it. | Delete the phrase entirely |
| "it is worth noting" | Same as above | Delete the phrase entirely |
| "ultimately" | AI conclusion tic | "At the end of the run", "The bottom line", or cut |
| "furthermore" / "moreover" | Academic AI voice | "Also", "Plus", or just start a new sentence |
| "in conclusion" | Robotic signposting | Natural ending — or no signpost at all |
| "first and foremost" | AI loves this. Humans never say it. | "First", "The main thing is" |
| "when it comes to" | Flabby AI transition | "For", "In", or restructure the sentence |
| "in terms of" | AI padding phrase | Usually deletable — try the sentence without it |
| "a testament to" | AI reviewer voice | "Shows", "Proves", or rephrase |
| "pivotal" | Overused in AI game writing | "Key", "Critical", "Essential" |
| "mastering the art of" | AI tutorial cliché | "Learning", "Getting good at" |
| "unleash the full potential" | AI hype voice | "Get the most out of", "Maximize" |
| "game-changer" | Lazy AI praise | Explain WHY it's good specifically |
| "elevate your gameplay" | AI guide cliché | "Play better", "Improve your runs" |
| "embark on a journey" | AI narrative voice | Just describe the game or build |
| "vibrant" / "bustling" | AI adjective spam | Use specific, concrete descriptions |

## Banned Patterns

### The AI Paragraph Sandwich
```
[Generic statement about the game]
[Specific point — the actual content]
[Generic conclusion restating the obvious]
```
**Fix:** Cut the first and last sentences. They add nothing.

### The AI Transition Crutch
Every paragraph starts with "Additionally,", "Moreover,", "However,", "Furthermore,"
**Fix:** Remove these transition words. Let paragraphs connect naturally through topic flow.

### The AI Uncertainty Hedge
"While there are many valid approaches..."
"Depending on your playstyle..."
"It's ultimately up to the player to decide..."
**Fix:** Take a stance. Pick the best option and say why. Readers want opinions, not equivocation.

### The AI List Exhaustion
Enumerating every single option without prioritization.
**Fix:** Rank, tier, or highlight the top picks. Readers don't need 12 options — they need the best 3-5.

## Read-Aloud Test

The single best detector: pick any paragraph and read it out loud.

- Does it sound like a person talking to you? → Good
- Does it sound like a Wikipedia article read by a text-to-speech bot? → Rewrite
- Can you imagine a streamer saying it on stream? → Good
- Does it sound like a college essay? → Rewrite

## Replacement Strategy

Don't just swap words — restructure the sentence entirely. AI-voice comes from sentence rhythm as much as vocabulary. If you find yourself changing "delve into" to "dig into" in an AI-sounding sentence, the whole sentence probably needs rewriting.

**Rule of thumb:** If you edit a sentence 3 times and it still sounds AI-ish, delete it and write a new one from scratch. It's faster.
